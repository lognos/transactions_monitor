# Interac e-Transfer Operating Rules & Threshold Governance

> **Payment Rail Scope:** `INTERAC_ETRANSFER`  
> **Statutory / Regulatory Authority:** `Interac Corp Participant Operating Regulations`  
> **Official Source:** [https://www.interac.ca/en/consumers/products/interac-e-transfer/](https://www.interac.ca/en/consumers/products/interac-e-transfer/)  
> **Jurisdiction:** Canada (Federal / Interac / Payments Canada / OSFI)  

---

## 1. Regulatory Mandate & Operational Summary
Establishes Interac e-Transfer transaction thresholds: standard retail limits of $3,000 / $5,000 CAD per 24 hours, commercial/business limits up to $10,000 CAD, rolling 7-day limits ($10,000 CAD) and 30-day limits ($30,000 CAD). Requires multi-factor customer authentication.

How-To: Send money withInterace-Transfer

How do you send money withInterace-Transfer? You and the recipient each need your own:

Account with a participating bank or credit union

Active email address or mobile phone number

To send, follow the easy-to-use instructions on screen:

Log into your online or mobile banking app and select the account.

Choose or add your recipient’s email or mobile number.

Enter the amount and a security question (no need if the recipient has registered forInterace-Transfer®Autodeposit).

That’s all. Just make sure you safely share the security answer with your recipient. Better yet, have your recipient register forInterace-Transfer Autodeposit for ease and added security.
