# Special Economic Measures Act (SEMA) & OSFI Sanctions Directives

> **Payment Rail Scope:** `WIRE`  
> **Statutory / Regulatory Authority:** `Special Economic Measures Act (S.C. 1992, c. 17) & OSFI Directives`  
> **Official Source:** [https://laws.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/S-14.5/index.html](https://laws.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/S-14.5/index.html)  
> **Jurisdiction:** Canada (Federal / Interac / Payments Canada / OSFI)  

---

## 1. Regulatory Mandate & Operational Summary
Statutory prohibition against dealing in property or facilitating financial transactions for sanctioned individuals, foreign states (Russia, Iran, Belarus, DPRK), and designated terrorists. Mandates immediate asset freezing and zero disbursement.

## 2. Deterministic Validation Thresholds & Constraints
- **Rule ID:** `SEMA-SANCT-01`
- **Category:** `SANCTIONS_ASSET_FREEZE`
- **Payment Rail:** `WIRE`
